# MarkOneFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

MarkOneFlow is a Chrome extension for viewing PDF files and taking notes. This policy explains what data the extension handles.

## Data we collect

MarkOneFlow does not collect, sell or share personal data. Your PDF files and notes are processed and stored locally in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers.

## Payments

The Pro version is sold through a third-party payment provider. Payment details are entered on the provider's page and are never seen or stored by MarkOneFlow. To activate Pro, the extension only checks your license status.

## Analytics and tracking

MarkOneFlow does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
